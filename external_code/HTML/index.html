Welcome to Salvo Metal Works

For years, Salvo Metal Works has been the premier destination for custom copper architectural fabrication in Naperville, Illinois, and the surrounding areas. We specialize in creating stunning, durable copper elements that enhance the aesthetic appeal and functionality of residential and commercial buildings.

Our expertise spans a comprehensive range of custom copper products, including chimney shrouds, cupolas, dormers, finials, leader boxes, roof vents, and snow guards. Each piece is meticulously crafted by our skilled artisans who understand the unique properties of copper and how to work with this beautiful metal to create architectural elements that will stand the test of time.

What sets Salvo Metal Works apart is our commitment to quality craftsmanship and attention to detail. We work closely with architects, contractors, and homeowners to bring their vision to life, ensuring that every custom copper piece not only meets but exceeds expectations. Our team combines traditional metalworking techniques with modern fabrication methods to deliver products that are both beautiful and functional.
