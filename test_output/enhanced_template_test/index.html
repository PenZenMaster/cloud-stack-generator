101 cupola

Cupolas are a sophisticated architectural feature often used in high-end homes to add both elegance and functionality to the roofline. These structures, typically perched at the highest point of the roof, are designed to enhance the homes exterior by introducing a timeless and refined element of design. Cupolas can be crafted in various shapes, including square, round, or octagonal, and are made from premium materials such as copper, lead-coated copper, freedom gray copper, zinc, or aluminum

301 finial

Originally crafted to solve water seepage issues on round roofs, finials have evolved into stunning decorative features, gracing iconic architectural styles from Gothic to Renaissance. At SMW, we take it a step further by offering customizable finials that not only enhance your structure’s visual appeal but also incorporate lightning protection systems for added safety. Trust in centuries of design influence with modern-day innovation.

501-slant-back-roof-vent vent

Roof vents serve two main purposes: air intake and exhaust. They come in many different shapes and sizes. Intakes are usually placed in the soffit area, but sometimes are placed on the roof near the eave. The exhaust vent is found near the ridge of the roof and is sometimes integrated into the roof system on top of the ridge.
